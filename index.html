Public Health Immunization Programme

Monitoring vaccination coverage and effects across regions

The challenge

A confidential public health client needed a clear, repeatable way to monitor immunisation uptake and see how vaccination effects differed between regions, so that teams could spot under-served areas instead of relying on scattered records.

My contribution
  • Explored and profiled the source tables, checking for duplicates, missing values and inconsistent region names.
  • Wrote SQL to join vaccination, population and region data and calculate coverage by region and period.
  • Compared regions side by side to highlight gaps and unusual patterns for stakeholder discussion.
  • Documented the logic so results could be re-run and understood by non-technical colleagues.
Tools & approach

SQL (joins, aggregation, CTEs, window functions) with a data-driven decision-making approach.

WITH cov AS (
  SELECT r.region_name,
         SUM(v.doses_given)  AS doses,
         MAX(p.eligible_pop) AS eligible
  FROM   vaccinations v
  JOIN   regions r    ON r.region_id = v.region_id
  JOIN   population p ON p.region_id = v.region_id
  GROUP  BY r.region_name)
SELECT region_name,
       ROUND(100.0*doses/eligible,1) AS coverage_pct,
       RANK() OVER (ORDER BY doses*1.0/eligible) AS gap_rank
FROM cov;
Outcome & learning

Delivered a documented set of queries and a regional comparison that made vaccination effects visible and easier to discuss. It strengthened my skills in data cleaning, validation and translating technical output into plain language for decision-makers.
